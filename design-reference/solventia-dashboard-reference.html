Roadmap

Make it official.

!Under 18? You will need a parent or guardian for registrations and the bank account. Each step says how.

  1. 01 Start as a sole proprietor The simplest way to begin. You trade in your own name and can move to a company later. When: now, while you test · no registration to file
  2. 02 Register as a small business (Udyam) A free online registration that gives your business an official identity. When: before your first paid booking · udyamregistration.gov.in
  3. 03 Open a current bank account Keeps business money separate from personal money, so your numbers stay honest. When: when the first payment is due · your bank
  4. 04 GST registration Whether you need it depends on your turnover and your type of business. The steps check your case. When: before you invoice, if your case needs it · gst.gov.in

General guidance, not legal or tax advice. Rules change. Each step shows the date it was last checked against the official site: [LAST CHECKED DATE]. Confirm with a CA before you register.
